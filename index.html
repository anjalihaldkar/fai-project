Who We Are

We build the farm,
then we make it produce.

Future Agri Infra develops turnkey polyhouse, nethouse and hydroponic farms across India — design, BOQ, subsidy filing, civil work, structure, fertigation and commissioning under one contract.

Where most contractors stop at handover, we stay: agronomy support through the first crop cycles, and so the produce sells at the price the model assumed.
