02 Explore Growing Systems

Three Growing Systems.
Multiple Possibilities.

Choose the right growing system based on your crop, location and business goals.

  1. 01

    Outdoor Hydroponics

    Soilless growing with optimized nutrient supply for higher yield and quality.

    • Higher productivity
    • Efficient water usage
    • Clean and uniform produce
  2. 02

    Cocopeat-Based Growing

    Sustainable and versatile growing system suitable for a wide range of crops.

    • Excellent root aeration
    • Good water retention
    • Ideal for high-value crops
  3. 03

    Soil-Based Growing

    Naturally grown with improved soil management for healthy and consistent yields.

    • Suitable for traditional crops
    • Cost-effective solution
    • Proven and reliable method
